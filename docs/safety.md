# Safety Guidelines

## Critical Safety Information

The PFGen system generates **high voltage pulses (500V - 1,700V)**. While the current is very low and the pulse duration is very short (40-50µs), these voltages can cause injury.

---

## Essential Safety Rules

1. **Never touch the circuit while powered on** — HV pulses are present on the PCB
2. **Always power down completely** before making any changes
3. **Use an inline fuse** on the supply line
4. **Keep one hand behind your back** when probing live circuits (prevents current path through heart)
5. **Use insulated probes** for all measurements
6. **The Neon bulb (U3)** provides HV bypass protection — ensure jumper H17 is correctly set
7. **Capacitors retain charge** — allow at least 1 hour after power-off before handling C4 and C5
8. **Work in a dry environment** — moisture increases shock risk
9. **Wear safety glasses** when soldering
10. **Keep a fire extinguisher accessible** — though risk is low, batteries can fail catastrophically

## Battery Safety

- Never short-circuit battery terminals
- Use matched batteries of the same chemistry, capacity, and age
- Monitor battery temperature during operation
- Discontinue use if batteries swell, leak, or become excessively hot
- Follow manufacturer guidelines for storage and disposal

## Soldering Safety

- Use adequate ventilation — solder fumes are irritating
- Use lead-free solder when possible
- Keep soldering iron in a stand when not in active use
- Allow components to cool before handling

## First Aid

In case of electric shock:
1. Do not touch the person — disconnect power first
2. Call emergency services
3. If safe, begin CPR if the person is not breathing
