# STEAMM Educational Framework

## What is STEAMM?

**S**cience · **T**echnology · **E**ducation · **A**rt · **M**athematics · **M**anufacturing

The IPC 5W Builder is the world's first complete STEAMM project — a single hands-on build that teaches across all six disciplines simultaneously.

## How Each Discipline Is Covered

### Science
- Electromagnetic theory and Faraday's Law
- Lenz's Law and back-EMF phenomena
- Thermodynamics and non-equilibrium systems
- Electrochemistry of battery systems
- The scientific method (hypothesis testing, CoP measurement)

### Technology
- PCB design and fabrication
- Electronic circuit analysis
- MOSFET/IGBT switching technology
- PWM signal generation
- Battery management systems

### Education
- Step-by-step guided learning with checkpoints
- Progressive complexity from theory to practice
- Self-directed exploration with measurement protocols
- Community knowledge sharing

### Art
- PCB layout aesthetics and design principles
- Clean wiring and system organization
- Visual communication through circuit diagrams
- Creative problem-solving in engineering constraints

### Mathematics
- Power calculations (P = V × I)
- CoP derivation and statistical analysis
- Uncertainty analysis and error propagation
- Angular velocity and torque calculations (for rotor systems)
- Thermodynamic equations for electrochemical systems

### Manufacturing
- PCB fabrication process
- Component sourcing and quality verification
- Soldering techniques and quality control
- Coil winding to specification
- System assembly and integration testing
