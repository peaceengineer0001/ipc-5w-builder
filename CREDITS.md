# Credits & Acknowledgments

The IPC (Inductive Pulse Charging) technology and educational framework are built upon decades of research and innovation by pioneering engineers and scientists.

## Project Lead

**Julian Perry** — Creator of the PFGen V5A(R) system, IPC.Engineer founder, and lead developer of the STEAMM educational curriculum for self-charging energy systems.

## Mentors & Influences

- **Peter Lindemann, D.Sc.** — Researcher and educator in advanced energy systems, whose work on radiant energy and motor design principles informed core IPC concepts.
- **Aaron Murakami** — Energy researcher, educator, and advocate for open-source energy technology sharing.
- **John C. Bedini** (1951–2016) — Pioneer of pulse charging technology and self-charging battery systems. His foundational work on inductor-based energy recovery is at the heart of IPC theory.
- **Robert Adams** (1920–2006) — New Zealand inventor whose Adams Motor demonstrated principles of back-EMF energy recovery that underpin modern IPC designs.

## Scientific Foundations

The IPC system draws on well-established electromagnetic principles:

- **Heinrich Lenz** — Lenz's Law governs the flyback pulse behavior central to inductive pulse charging.
- **Michael Faraday** — Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction provides the theoretical basis for energy transfer in IPC coils.
- **Nikola Tesla** — Tesla's pioneering work on resonance, inductive coupling, and pulsed energy systems inspired generations of energy researchers.
